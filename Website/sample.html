How > System Works

Our AI pipeline combines prompt-engineered LLMs with real job postings to map your goals, recommend essential skills, and generate a realistic learning plan that fits your available time and preferred role.

01
Profile & Goal Intake

You share your background, preferred role (frontend, backend, data analyst, etc.), and preparation time. The system builds a tailored persona for precise guidance.

02
Skill Recommendation Engine

Using curated job data and LLM reasoning, the agent selects only the most impactful skills and tools for your target role, removing unnecessary or low-impact topics.

03
Learning Plan & Evaluation

A month-by-month learning roadmap is generated with checkpoints. Adaptive question–answer sessions then test your knowledge and update your readiness level.
